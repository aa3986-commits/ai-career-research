# AI Product Manager

## What does this person do?

An AI Product Manager helps a team decide which AI features to build and why customers need them.

## Typical responsibilities

- Research customer problems.
- Write product requirements.
- Prioritize features and plan releases.
- Work with designers and engineers.
- Measure results and manage risks.

## Useful skills

Communication, product planning, data analysis, understanding AI limits, privacy awareness, and teamwork.

## Portfolio project

Write a product plan for an AI career-advice app. Include user needs, features, sample screens, risks, and measures of success.
